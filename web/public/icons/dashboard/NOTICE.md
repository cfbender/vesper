# Bundled service icons

The PNG files in this directory are from Homarr Labs Dashboard Icons
(https://github.com/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons), commit
`d767d7327db7f8ebd3715b552523f61889eea41c`, used under the Apache License,
Version 2.0 (see `LICENSE` in this directory).

Modifications: a curated subset was taken from the upstream `png/` directory
(where upstream offers light and dark variants, the brighter one is used for
legibility on Vesper's dark background), resized to 128×128 with transparent padding, and
stripped of metadata. Files are renamed to the catalog ID listed in
`../catalog.json`.

Trademark notice: all product names, logos, trademarks and registered
trademarks are the property of their respective owners. They are used only to
identify the services being monitored and do not imply endorsement of or
affiliation with Vesper.

Vesper serves these files locally; it makes no runtime CDN requests.
